War of the Ring: Sieges of Middle-earth Announced

2026-09-21

Ares Games has revealed Sieges of Middle-earth, the next expansion for its definitive grand strategy title, War of the Ring. The upcoming release focuses specifically on the protracted assaults across Tolkien's realm, introducing dedicated mechanics and bespoke components tailored to fortress defense and siege warfare.

In the core game, strongholds like Helm’s Deep and Minas Tirith serve as operational anchors—often turning into attrition-heavy bottlenecks that dictate the Shadow’s victory timeline. By isolating and deepening the mechanics of prolonged sieges, Ares looks to move beyond brute-force die rolling to offer richer tactical friction during stronghold assaults.

While complete component breakdowns and release dates are yet to be disclosed, the design direction is clear. For seasoned players, this promises a calibrated shift in how territory is held, contested, and ultimately breached.
