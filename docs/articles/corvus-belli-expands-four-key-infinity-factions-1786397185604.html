Corvus Belli Expands Four Key Infinity Factions

2026-08-10

Corvus Belli has unveiled a fresh wave of reinforcements for its flagship sci-fi skirmish wargame, Infinity. The upcoming release cycle introduces key unit expansions and sectorial additions across four distinct factions: PanOceania, Yu Jing, the Nomads, and the newly established Shindenbutai.

Headlining the drop is the Kestrel Colonial Force, offering PanOceania commanders new flexibility in list building and fireteam composition. Meanwhile, Yu Jing and Nomads receive specialized operatives tailored for mid-board dominance and order pool efficiency, while Shindenbutai gets crucial support to solidify its unique footprint within the Human Sphere.

In an asymmetric skirmish system where individual order economy and reactive play dictate the tempo, even subtle profile additions can redefine competitive metas. By offering nuanced tactical tools across both established powerhouses and newer sectorials, Corvus Belli is actively encouraging players to rethink board control and asset deployment. For veteran commanders, it is a welcome invitation to re-evaluate their opening moves.
