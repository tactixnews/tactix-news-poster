WizKids Unveils Official Dark Sun Miniature Line

2026-08-09

WizKids has announced a new line of officially licensed miniatures based on Dark Sun, one of Dungeons & Dragons' most notoriously unforgiving campaign settings. The upcoming collection brings the scorched world of Athas back to physical grid maps with detailed sculpts tailored for survival-focused play.

First introduced in 1991, Dark Sun stands apart from traditional high fantasy by emphasizing environmental scarcity, psionics, and oppressive tyrannical rule. On the tabletop, tactical engagement in Athas demands a distinct visual clarity, where terrain hazards, resource tracking, and positional advantage dictate survival far more than standard magical abundance.

While Wizards of the Coast has remained deliberate with official setting updates, WizKids' release highlights an enduring appetite for high-stakes, survivalist TTRPG campaigns. For strategic game masters, these miniatures provide the essential physical presence required to run truly grueling, atmospheric encounters.
