Games Workshop Unveils Grand Cathay Army Boxes

2026-08-03

Games Workshop is expanding Warhammer: The Old World with two new Battle March Army boxed sets, officially introducing Grand Cathay to physical rank-and-flank play alongside a formidable Warriors of Chaos release. This marks the physical tabletop debut for the dragon-ruled empire, a faction that previously found its fullest expression in digital form.

For tabletop strategists, Cathay’s arrival offers more than aesthetic variety. The faction introduces a distinct doctrine rooted in tactical harmony, balancing disciplined missile lines, heavy cavalry, and celestial magic. Bringing these mechanics to physical movement trays forces players to reconsider battleline spacing and combined-arms synergy in an evolving metagame.

Paired with the aggressive, front-line presence of the Warriors of Chaos set, this expansion demonstrates Games Workshop’s commitment to deepening the game's competitive and narrative scope. The balance of power in the Old World is shifting, and commanders must adapt.
