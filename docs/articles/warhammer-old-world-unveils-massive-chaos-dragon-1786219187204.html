Warhammer Old World Unveils Massive Chaos Dragon

2026-08-08

Games Workshop has officially opened pre-orders for the Warriors of Chaos faction in Warhammer: The Old World, anchored by a striking dual-headed Chaos Dragon kit. This release expands the game's fantasy range significantly, delivering a wave of new battalion army bundles, specialized unit kits, and essential rules options for tabletop commanders.

The centerpiece Chaos Dragon represents more than just a painting benchmark; it serves as a formidable tactical asset. Configurable as either a Lord of Chaos or a Chaos Sorcerer Lord, the dual-headed beast introduces immense high-mobility threat projection into the rank-and-flank environment. Its presence on the board forces opponents to reevaluate deployment geometry and dedicate high-yield counter-play early.

For tabletop strategists, this release signals a clear shift in how Chaos armies will maneuver on the battlefield. By pairing a high-impact centerpiece monster with foundational army boxes and specialized regiment kits, Games Workshop provides accessible entry points for new commanders alongside deep list-building flexibility for veteran generals looking to dictate the tempo of engagement.
