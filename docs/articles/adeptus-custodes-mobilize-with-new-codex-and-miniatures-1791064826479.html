Adeptus Custodes Mobilize with New Codex and Miniatures

2026-10-03

Games Workshop has opened pre-orders for the latest Adeptus Custodes range, delivering a dedicated codex alongside an updated suite of miniature kits for the Emperor's bodyguard. The launch updates the faction's competitive foundation, coupling refined detachment mechanics with modernized sculpts for the Imperium's most elite infantry.

For Custodes commanders, this revision arrives at a critical tactical juncture. Defined by hyper-elite profiles and low model counts, the army has consistently balanced formidable per-model durability against severe objective control deficits. The new rules framework seeks to mitigate these vulnerabilities, introducing operational versatility that rewards disciplined board positioning rather than brute-force attrition.

Alongside the strategic updates, the refreshed sculpts bring sharper silhouette clarity and dynamic posing to the golden vanguard. As lists begin to circulate across competitive circuits, the true test will be whether these updated detachment rules provide the adaptability required to navigate today's lethal, combined-arms meta.
