Stonemaier Games Reveals Fantasy Euro 'Braided Skies'

2026-09-23

Stonemaier Games has formally unveiled Braided Skies, an upcoming heavy euro design centered on airship navigation, trade logistics, and route optimization across floating cloud islands. Set within the high-altitude fantasy setting of the Smoking Bones universe, the standalone title marks the publisher's newest foray into high-complexity worker-placement systems.

At its strategic core, Braided Skies tasks players with commanding and iteratively upgrading modular skyships. Gameplay revolves around ferrying travelers, fulfilling high-yield contracts, and navigating shifting aerial pathways that evolve over the course of the session. Rather than relying on a static board state, the game's variable geography forces players to constantly balance short-term tactical pickups against long-range engine efficiency.

For Stonemaier, best known for modern classics like Scythe and Wingspan, Braided Skies signals a clear commitment to intricate, systems-first design. In an increasingly crowded worker-placement market, the game's dynamic routing mechanisms and layered logistics offer a promising operational challenge for dedicated strategy enthusiasts.
