Steve Jackson Steps Down After 46 Years

2026-09-12

After nearly half a century at the helm, Steve Jackson has officially stepped down as chief executive officer of Steve Jackson Games. Sabrina Gonzalez, a longtime veteran within the company, assumes the chief executive role, marking the end of Jackson's 46-year tenure guiding the operational side of one of tabletop gaming's most resilient independent publishers.

The leadership transition is less a retirement and more a strategic realignment. By offloading administrative, financial, and operational responsibilities to Gonzalez, Jackson is positioning himself to focus exclusively on game design. For a developer whose portfolio spans landmark titles like Ogre, Car Wars, Munchkin, and the foundational modular architecture of GURPS, the move frees up critical creative bandwidth.

In an industry climate defined by studio consolidation and complex supply chains, this handover provides structural continuity without sacrificing creative identity. Elevating internal leadership preserves institutional knowledge while liberating the studio's primary designer from executive friction—a calculation that could yield a compelling late-career renaissance for one of hobby gaming's foundational minds.
