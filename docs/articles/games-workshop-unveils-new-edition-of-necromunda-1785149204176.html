Games Workshop Unveils New Edition of Necromunda

2026-07-27

Games Workshop has confirmed a new edition of Necromunda, its gang-based skirmish game set in the underhive of Hive Primus. The update brings refined campaign systems and modernized skirmish rules aimed at smoothing out some of the friction points longtime players have flagged over the years.

Early details point to streamlined turn structure and clearer scaling for multi-gang campaigns, without abandoning the grim, scrappy identity that's defined Necromunda since its introduction. For a game built on escalating rivalries between gangs, the campaign-system changes look like the more consequential shift here.

TACTIX will have a full breakdown as more concrete rules details surface. For now: expect existing gangs to remain playable, with the new edition framed as a refinement rather than a replacement.
