GW Revives Classic Rogue Trader Ogryns

2026-09-28

Games Workshop has unveiled three upcoming miniature releases for House Orlock in Necromunda, drawing direct aesthetic lineage from the classic Rogue Trader era. The forthcoming wave introduces rugged frontline gangers alongside cybernetically augmented Ogryn minders, engineered specifically to endure the punishing attrition of industrial skirmishes.

The kits arrive alongside an upcoming Underhive Journal supplement, which delivers expanded lore and dedicated rulesets for fielding these heavy minders. Mechanically, the additions offer Orlock players critical tactical versatility, providing high-toughness counter-charge anchors to shield the house's signature medium-range ballistic firepower.

Reaching back to foundational late-1980s sculpts is a deliberate design choice that honors veteran hobbyists while offering newer players distinctive silhouette variety on the tabletop. Necromunda continues to serve as Games Workshop's most expressive narrative platform, demonstrating that heritage aesthetics and modern skirmish depth can coexist seamlessly.
