Wizards of the Coast Launches Exodus Sci-Fi RPG

2026-09-10

Wizards of the Coast has officially released Exodus, a standalone sci-fi tabletop roleplaying game developed in tandem with Archetype Entertainment’s forthcoming AAA video game. Debuting with the Traveler’s Handbook and the lore-focused Exodus Encyclopedia, the system establishes a new, high-concept setting spearheaded by veteran BioWare designer James Ohlen.

For Wizards of the Coast, this release represents a rare and deliberate divergence from traditional fantasy. While Dungeons & Dragons remains the dominant force in tabletop fantasy, Exodus embraces hard space-opera mechanics, emphasizing themes of time dilation, planetary survival, and the cost of human diaspora. Rather than retrofitting existing fantasy engines, the design focuses on setting-specific stakes tailored to cross-media storytelling.

Building a tabletop system alongside an unreleased video game is an ambitious strategic gambit. If Exodus captures an audience at the table before its digital counterpart arrives, it could offer WotC a proven blueprint for launching modern, original intellectual property far beyond the shadow of the d20 fantasy canon.
