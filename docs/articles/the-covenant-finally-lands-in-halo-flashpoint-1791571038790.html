The Covenant Finally Lands in Halo: Flashpoint

2026-10-09

Mantic Games has officially unveiled the Covenant as the next major faction for Halo: Flashpoint, anchoring the upcoming Judgement expansion. The reveal introduces the franchise's definitive alien adversaries to the skirmish tabletop, debuting miniature sculpts for both Sangheili Elites and Unggoy Grunts.

Since Flashpoint debuted in 2024, the skirmish system has operated primarily through Spartan-versus-Spartan War Games simulations. While mechanically tight, that mirror-match foundation left a glaring thematic absence at the center of the sandbox. The arrival of the Covenant fundamentally transforms the game from an intramural training exercise into an authentic asymmetric conflict.

Strategically, the shift introduces fresh tactical profiles to test Flashpoint's streamlined ruleset. Balancing durable, energy-shielded Elites with expendable swarms of Grunts will alter board control, activation economy, and target priority. For commanders accustomed to matching Spartan armor against Spartan armor, adapting to non-human squad hierarchies marks the beginning of Flashpoint's true competitive era.
