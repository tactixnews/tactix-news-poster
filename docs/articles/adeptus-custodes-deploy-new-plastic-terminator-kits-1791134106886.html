Adeptus Custodes Deploy New Plastic Terminator Kits

2026-10-04

Games Workshop has officially opened pre-orders for standalone plastic Terminator kits for the Adeptus Custodes, releasing elite heavy armor units previously restricted to bundled box sets. The rollout gives Golden Throne commanders direct access to core heavy infantry without relying on secondary market markups or multi-faction starter boxes.

For Custodes players, accessibility heavily influences list design. Terminators serve as critical deep-strike threats and character hunters, engineered to crack fortified positions and hold contested primary objectives under punishing fire. Decoupling these sculpts from high-barrier bundles allows generals to fine-tune unit sizes and experiment with armored detachments as the competitive meta shifts.

Beyond simple convenience, the standalone release stabilizes the faction's tactical ecosystem. When essential high-durability assets become modular and readily available in multipart plastic, army construction pivots away from restrictive workarounds toward genuine strategic flexibility.
