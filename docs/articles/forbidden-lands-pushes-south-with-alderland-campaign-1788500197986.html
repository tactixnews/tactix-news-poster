Forbidden Lands Pushes South with Alderland Campaign

2026-09-04

Free League Publishing has opened pre-orders for Alderland, the latest major campaign expansion for its survival-driven tabletop roleplaying game, Forbidden Lands. Shifting the spotlight south toward a fractured human kingdom, the release places players directly into a landscape under siege, where the secretive Silent Knights maintain fortified lines against gathering non-human hosts.

True to the core system's tactical identity, Alderland relies on emergent, site-based exploration rather than scripted narrative paths. The new regional sandbox introduces modular encounter tables, distinct faction agendas, and deep geopolitical friction. Game masters gain access to structural tools designed to track escalating conflicts, requiring adventuring parties to balance scarce resource management with delicate local allegiances.

Since its launch, Forbidden Lands has distinguished itself by pairing old-school hexcrawl grit with modern, friction-heavy dice mechanics. Alderland marks an ambitious expansion of that strategic framework, widening the physical and political scope of the setting without diluting the brutal, ground-level survival that defines the game.
