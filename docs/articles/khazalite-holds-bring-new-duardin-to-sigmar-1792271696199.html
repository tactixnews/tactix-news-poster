Khazalite Holds Bring New Duardin to Sigmar

2026-10-17

Games Workshop has opened pre-orders for the Khazalite Holds, introducing a distinct new Duardin faction to Warhammer Age of Sigmar. The initial rollout centers on a comprehensive army set, packaged alongside warscroll cards and an exclusive alternate-cover battletome that establishes the tactical identity of this heavily armored roster.

Mechanically, the Khazalite Holds fill an enduring niche in the Mortal Realms. Where Fyreslayers rely on unarmored fury and Kharadron Overlords excel in mobile aerial skirmishing, the Khazalite doctrine revives traditional dwarven warfare: impenetrable defensive lines, grinding attritional endurance, and crushing artillery support. Their battlefield presence prioritizes positional integrity and layered armor saves over rapid maneuvers.

This expansion signals a deliberate shift toward methodical board control in a game often dictated by high-tempo alpha strikes. For competitive players, the strategic challenge will center on objective play—specifically whether these resilient infantry blocks can outlast high-mobility lists and lock down primary scoring zones through pure stubborn attrition.
