Alpha Black Lotus Sells for $2.2 Million

2026-09-20

A pristine PSA 10 copy of Magic: The Gathering's foundational Alpha Black Lotus has commanded over $2.2 million at public auction, cementing its standing among the most valuable cultural artifacts in modern tabletop history. The historic transaction marks one of the highest public hammer prices ever recorded for a collectible card game asset.

While private dealer networks have occasionally brokered higher numbers, public gavel results provide rare, verified transparency into a secondary market often shrouded in non-disclosure agreements. Designed by Richard Garfield and illustrated by the late Christopher Rush, the 1993 Alpha printing had an estimated run of only 1,100 copies. Pristine, unplayed survivors represent a vanishingly small fraction of that total, creating a structural supply floor that institutional capital continues to chase.

For the broader industry, the $2.2 million benchmark reinforces a structural shift: blue-chip gaming history is now assessed through the same preservationist lens as fine art or rare numismatics. The Alpha Black Lotus is no longer regarded simply as an overpowered mana engine, but as the foundational genesis artifact of modern strategy gaming.
