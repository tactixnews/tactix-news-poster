Dark Horse Expands Acclaimed Lancer TTRPG Line

2026-08-29

Dark Horse Books and Massif Press have officially partnered to publish two new physical sourcebooks for Lancer, the critically acclaimed tactical mech tabletop RPG. The upcoming releases will include Operation Fulcrum Dawn, an introductory narrative module, alongside the expansive LANCER: Setting Guide Vol. 1.

Since its debut, Lancer has carved out a dedicated following by balancing high-crunch, grid-based tactical skirmishes with rules-light, narrative roleplay outside the cockpit. Partnering with a major publisher like Dark Horse marks a pivotal transition for Massif Press, scaling the indie darling into mainstream tabletop distribution channels without sacrificing its signature design ethos.

Operation Fulcrum Dawn is engineered to lower the barrier to entry for new Game Masters, delivering ready-to-run combat encounters and mission frameworks. In parallel, the Setting Guide Vol. 1 provides comprehensive lore on the Union and the peripheral systems, giving pilots and tacticians greater narrative depth and strategic context for every drop.
