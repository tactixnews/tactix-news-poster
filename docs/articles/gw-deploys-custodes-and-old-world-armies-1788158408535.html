GW Deploys Custodes and Old World Armies

2026-08-31

Games Workshop has officially launched pre-orders for a dual-front release wave, introducing heavy armored reinforcements for the Adeptus Custodes in Warhammer 40,000 alongside five comprehensive Battle March army sets for Warhammer: The Old World.

For Custodes commanders, these reinforced units offer critical answers to an evolving competitive meta. Elite, low-model-count armies depend heavily on durable anchor units to dictate board control. Expanding the golden host's armored roster provides fresh operational flexibility without diluting the faction's signature elite profile.

Across the fantasy divide, the five Battle March sets mark a significant structural push for The Old World. Assembling traditional rank-and-flank forces has historically posed a steep barrier to entry; these curated formations establish an accessible foundation for newcomers while offering veterans an efficient path to scale existing battlelines.

By simultaneously reinforcing its flagship sci-fi arena and its heritage fantasy system, Games Workshop continues to support distinct strategic philosophies across the tabletop landscape.
