Wizards of the Coast Staff Launch Historic Union Bid

2026-10-07

Employees across Wizards of the Coast have formally announced their intent to unionize, marking one of the most consequential labor developments in modern tabletop gaming history. The organizing effort unites talent across Dungeons & Dragons, Magic: The Gathering, and supporting digital operations under a collective bargaining framework.

At the core of the campaign are three critical priorities: equitable compensation, protected creative stewardship over legacy franchises, and definitive workplace safeguards against generative artificial intelligence. As Hasbro increasingly relies on Wizards as its primary profit engine, the staff responsible for driving that value are demanding structural influence over the tools and conditions shaping their work.

This movement signals a maturing industry confronting the limits of passion-driven labor. By challenging corporate consolidation and addressing the existential threat of automated design, the Wizards organizing committee is doing more than renegotiating contracts—they are establishing a precedent that could redefine the economics of tabletop publishing worldwide.
