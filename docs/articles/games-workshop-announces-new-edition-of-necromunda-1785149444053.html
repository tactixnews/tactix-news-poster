Games Workshop Announces New Edition of Necromunda

2026-07-27

Games Workshop is officially refreshing its premier sci-fi skirmish system with the announcement of Necromunda Skirmish. Scheduled for pre-order next week, this latest edition consolidates years of mechanical evolution into a revamped core rulebook, accompanied by a major launch box featuring redesigned gang miniatures and dense modular terrain.

For veteran commanders of the Underhive, a new edition represents more than just fresh sculpts; it offers a long-overdue streamlining of Necromunda’s complex rules ecosystem. Over recent years, tracking campaign progression, injury tables, and gang-specific gear required navigating multiple expansion supplements. By unifying these mechanics, Games Workshop aims to lower the barrier to entry while refining the deep tactical asymmetry that defines hive city warfare.

The focus on refreshed gang miniatures and modular terrain signals a continued commitment to high-density, vertical skirmish design. As pre-orders go live across major retail channels, the true test for Necromunda Skirmish will lie in how balance is maintained across disparate gang archetypes without sacrificing the emergent, narrative-driven campaign play that has anchored the tabletop experience for decades.
