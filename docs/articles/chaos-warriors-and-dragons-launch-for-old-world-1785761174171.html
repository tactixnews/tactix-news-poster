Chaos Warriors and Dragons Launch for Old World

2026-08-03

Games Workshop has unveiled the next major release wave for Warhammer: The Old World, bringing the forces of Chaos to the forefront of rank-and-flank strategy. The announcement spotlights an extensive overhaul for the dark factions, introducing new plastic sculpts for essential frontline units alongside centerpiece monstrous threats.

Leading the advance are redesigned Chaos Warriors and Chaos Knights, retaining their signature heavy armor aesthetic while incorporating updated sculpting standards. Complementing these shock troops is a multi-headed Chaos Dragon, designed as both a tactical apex predator and a visual centerpiece capable of disrupting enemy lines through raw damage output and terror mechanics.

From a strategic perspective, this expansion injects significant shock-cavalry power and durable infantry back into the meta. General players must now account for high-armor advance vectors that can absorb ranged fire while closing distance rapidly. As the Old World competitive scene continues to mature, this wave forces every faction commander to re-evaluate their battlelines against pure brute force.
