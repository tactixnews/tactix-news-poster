Games Workshop Reveals Redesigned Warhammer 40k Ork Nobz

2026-08-11

Games Workshop has previewed updated miniature sculpts for the Ork Nobz, bringing modernized proportions and refined aesthetic details to one of Warhammer 40,000’s most recognizable heavy infantry units. The overhaul replaces an aging kit, introducing dynamic posing options and scale consistency that better reflect their battlefield role.

The new models address long-standing scale inflation across the game, aligning Ork squad leaders with modern miniature design standards. Beyond visual appeal, the elevated sculpt definition and distinct silhouettes enhance army readability on the tabletop. In high-density force lists, clear visual hierarchy provides immediate tactical clarity for both players during complex movement phases.

More significantly, the redesign signals a broader range update for the Ork faction within the current edition. Redesigning foundational infantry kits often precedes systemic rules revisions and codex support. For competitive players and collectors alike, this update suggests that Orks are positioned for a meaningful strategic evolution in the months ahead.
