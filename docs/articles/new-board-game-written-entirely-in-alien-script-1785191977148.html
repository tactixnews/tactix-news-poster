New Board Game Written Entirely in Alien Script

2026-07-27

City of Six Moons presents players with an unprecedented intellectual hurdle before the first component ever touches the board: its entire rulebook is written in an invented, untranslated alien language. Omitted from the box is any official translation key, forcing players to decipher the game's underlying mechanics purely through structural analysis, visual logic, and contextual clues.

This radical approach transforms rule comprehension into the game's initial strategic layer. Rather than treating instructions as a passive barrier to entry, the design elevates linguistic deduction into a core mechanic. Players must act as cryptographers, forming hypotheses about turn structures, resource loops, and victory conditions, then validating those theories through iterative play.

By withholding a definitive manual, City of Six Moons relies entirely on emergent consensus and collective intelligence within the tabletop community. It stands as a provocative experiment in game design, challenging our assumptions about how strategic systems are communicated—and proving that the most compelling puzzles sometimes begin before the board is even set.
