Free League Announces Throne of Thorns II Expansion

2026-08-09

Free League Publishing has officially unveiled The Throne of Thorns II, advancing the central campaign arc for Ruins of Symbaroum 5E. Pre-orders are currently live on the publisher's webshop, granting immediate early access to the alpha PDF for players eager to map their next venture into the depths of Davokar.

The new volume escalates the tension within the ancient, corrupted forest, blending high-stakes factional politics with grueling wilderness survival. What sets Symbaroum apart in the saturated 5E landscape is its deliberate subversion of traditional power fantasy. Free League embeds a lethal, atmosphere-heavy framework into fifth-edition mechanics, forcing parties to weigh resource management and tactical positioning against corruptive forces.

This release marks a critical continuation of Free League’s effort to bridge deep narrative design with accessible mechanics. By adapting one of tabletop roleplaying's most revered dark fantasy sagas for the 5E system, the studio provides Game Masters with a masterclass in atmospheric tension and strategic storytelling.
