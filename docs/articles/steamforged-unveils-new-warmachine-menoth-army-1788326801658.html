Steamforged Unveils New Warmachine Menoth Army

2026-09-02

Steamforged Games has revealed the Covenant of the Flame, delivering a comprehensive preview of the reimagined army that formally brings the iconic Protectorate of Menoth back to Warmachine.

The reveal offers commanders a detailed look at the faction's aesthetic and systemic transition under Steamforged's stewardship. Historically defined by interlocking defensive auras, layered buffs, and relentless fire damage, the faction's new sculpts honor its holy zealot heritage while adopting the sleeker, modular design standards of the game's current era.

For veteran tacticians, the Covenant represents a critical milestone in the revival of the Iron Kingdoms. It signals that Steamforged intends to respect the distinct mechanical identities of legacy armies while recalibrating their footprint for a faster, more dynamic competitive meta.
