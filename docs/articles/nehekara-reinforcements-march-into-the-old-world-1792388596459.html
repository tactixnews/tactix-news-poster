Nehekara Reinforcements March Into The Old World

2026-10-19

Games Workshop is expanding its support for Warhammer: The Old World with a substantial wave of Tomb Kings of Khemri reinforcements. The upcoming release centers on a new army box alongside a companion Arcane Journal, providing Nehekharan generals with an influx of skeletal infantry, swift chariot formations, and expanded tactical profiles.

For Tomb Kings commanders, this wave addresses both tabletop presence and strategic flexibility. The emphasis on modular infantry cadres and high-mobility chariot forces reinforces Nehekara's defining archetype: steady, grinding attrition paired with sudden flanking pressure. Rather than relying purely on centerpiece constructs, players gain the tools to field cohesive, massed battlelines that force opponents to manage threat ranges across the entire width of the board.

The accompanying Arcane Journal will likely prove just as consequential. These supplements have become essential to The Old World's evolving ecosystem, delivering specialized Armies of Infamy, bespoke magic items, and distinct unit variants. As the tactical landscape continues to settle, Nehekara's updated playbook offers commanders a sharper set of options to contest the movement phase and control the tempo of battle.
