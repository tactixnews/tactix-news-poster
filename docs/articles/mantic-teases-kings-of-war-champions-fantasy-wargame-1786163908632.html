Mantic Teases Kings of War Champions Fantasy Wargame

2026-08-08

Mantic Games has officially teased Kings of War: Champions, a new entry in its flagship fantasy ecosystem scheduled for release next month. Positioned as a faster, character-centric evolution of traditional rank-and-flank wargaming, the title shifts strategic emphasis onto legendary commanders leading specialized regiment formations.

Historically, mass-battle fantasy systems struggle to balance macro-level troop positioning with high-impact heroics. By streamlining unit mechanics and focusing army synergy around individual champions, Mantic is targeting a compelling middle ground: the spatial discipline of regimented warfare combined with the direct tactical agency of skirmish design.

Full mechanical previews and unit showcases will roll out through March and April ahead of the official launch. For wargamers seeking a tighter, command-driven alternative to traditional attrition-heavy battle systems, Champions marks an ambitious step forward for Mantic's core design philosophy.
