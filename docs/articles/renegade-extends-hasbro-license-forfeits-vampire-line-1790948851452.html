Renegade Extends Hasbro License, Forfeits Vampire Line

2026-10-02

Renegade Game Studios has renewed its licensing partnership with Hasbro, securing the future of its Transformers, G.I. Joe, and Power Rangers lines while committing future releases to compatibility with the revised Dungeons & Dragons 5.5E ruleset. Concurrently, the studio confirmed it will conclude its tenure with Paradox Interactive, returning the World of Darkness publishing banner directly to White Wolf.

The decision represents a clear strategic consolidation. By aligning its core licensed properties with the updated 5E framework, Renegade guarantees its titles remain directly plugged into tabletop gaming's largest and most lucrative player base. For Hasbro, extending this deal maintains a healthy secondary ecosystem around their legacy brands without requiring direct internal development costs from Wizards of the Coast.

Conversely, shedding the World of Darkness portfolio relieves Renegade of a notoriously complex editorial burden. Renegade assumed publishing duties for Vampire: The Masquerade and Hunter: The Reckoning in late 2020 following internal restructuring at Paradox. With White Wolf reclaiming operational control over the gothic-punk universe, Renegade trades high-maintenance legacy lore for the reliable momentum of mainstream d20 design.
