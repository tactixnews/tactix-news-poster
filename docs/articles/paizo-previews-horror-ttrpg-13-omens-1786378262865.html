Paizo Previews Horror TTRPG 13 Omens

2026-08-10

Paizo is stepping outside the high-fantasy boundary of Pathfinder with 13 Omens, an upcoming tabletop roleplaying game focused on psychological dread and survival. Previewed at Gen Con, the title trades heroic power fantasies for intimate, investigative horror set against lethal supernatural threats.

Early impressions reveal a mechanical framework engineered explicitly to build escalating tension. Rather than relying on power scaling and tactical combat loops, 13 Omens measures success through resource management, sanity, and the weight of creeping dread. Every decision feels intentionally volatile.

For Paizo, this pivot represents a calculated expansion into a market segment long dominated by investigative horror titles. By designing a system centered on vulnerability rather than heroic resilience, the studio is testing whether its signature design depth can capture the nuances of atmospheric terror.

Whether 13 Omens can balance narrative dread with engaging mechanical choices remains to be seen, but its early framework points to a deliberate, sophisticated departure from traditional TTRPG design.
