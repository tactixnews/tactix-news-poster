GW Reveals Exclusive Darkoath Warqueen for Warhammer Day

2026-10-05

Games Workshop has unveiled its commemorative release for Warhammer Day, showcasing a striking new sculpt for the Slaves to Darkness Darkoath Warqueen. Wielding an oversized greataxe and draped in northern pelts and macabre trophies, the limited-edition miniature is slated to headline the studio's annual preview festivities.

The miniature represents a deliberate aesthetic shift within the Mortal Realms, leaning heavily into raw, uncompromising physicality rather than ornate Chaos iconography. With dense musculature and an imposing, grounded stance, the design reinforces the Darkoath's distinctive identity as desperate, hardened survivors carving out their existence through sheer martial willpower.

Beyond its collector appeal, the release serves as a visual anchor for Darkoath-focused Slaves to Darkness armies. As Games Workshop continues to expand the sub-faction's dedicated range, this sculpt offers hobbyists and commanders a high-impact centerpiece that instantly communicates the brutal ethos of the northern tribes.
