Steve Jackson Steps Down as SJG CEO

2026-09-12

After more than four decades steering one of the industry's defining independent publishers, Steve Jackson is stepping aside from executive management. The founder of Steve Jackson Games has officially transitioned out of the Chief Executive Officer role, handing operational leadership to a newly appointed executive team while shifting his daily focus entirely back to game design and development.

The handover closes a foundational chapter in tabletop history. Founded in 1980, the studio produced enduring staples across multiple formats, from the tactical grid combat of Car Wars and the universal systems of GURPS to the retail phenomenon of Munchkin. While steering business operations and navigating historic legal battles defined his executive tenure, Jackson's core legacy has always been rooted in systems architecture and inventive rule design.

Executive succession is notoriously difficult for founder-driven studios, but stripping away administrative friction may serve as a major strategic asset. In a tabletop market increasingly dominated by rapid release cycles and visual spectacle, re-anchoring one of hobby gaming's sharpest mechanical minds at the design table suggests an intriguing creative resurgence for the publisher.
