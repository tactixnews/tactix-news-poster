Netflix Developing D&D Ravenloft Series With Cuarón

2026-09-03

Netflix has entered development on a live-action series set within Ravenloft, Dungeons & Dragons' enduring gothic horror setting. Filmmakers John August and Academy Award-winner Alfonso Cuarón are attached to the project alongside Hasbro, marking a decisive pivot away from previously planned television adaptations set in the high-fantasy Forgotten Realms, which have reportedly been shelved.

The strategic shift signals a calculated recalibration for Wizards of the Coast. Rather than competing directly in a crowded prestige landscape dominated by sprawling, open-world fantasy epics, Ravenloft offers a distinct thematic canvas. Leaning into psychological dread, isolated domains of terror, and iconic figures like Count Strahd von Zarovich allows the production to trade generic spectacle for focused, atmospheric horror.

Enlisting Cuarón and August points to an ambition that transcends routine IP mining. By approaching tabletop lore through the lens of auteur-driven filmmaking, Netflix and Hasbro appear ready to treat D&D not merely as a franchise of rules and monsters, but as a framework for genuine cinematic narrative.
