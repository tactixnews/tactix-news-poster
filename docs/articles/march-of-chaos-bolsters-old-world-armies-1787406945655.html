March of Chaos Bolsters Old World Armies

2026-08-22

Games Workshop is expanding faction design in Warhammer: The Old World with the upcoming March of Chaos supplement, introducing The Hordes of Chaos as the game's newest Army of Infamy. The release refines list building for Chaos commanders, delivering dedicated rules and bespoke unit interactions tailored for both competitive and narrative battlefields.

Armies of Infamy have quickly become the primary mechanism for deepening tactical identity within The Old World. Rather than relying on straightforward stat adjustments, these thematic sub-factions reframe structural decision-making on the tabletop. For Chaos generals, this shift encourages flexible, combined-arms compositions over traditional, singular hammer units, opening fresh avenues for board control and momentum management.

As the meta continues to mature, March of Chaos underscores a deliberate effort to reward nuanced army composition. How effectively these new synergies withstand disciplined rank-and-flank opposition will define the faction's standing in the seasons ahead.
