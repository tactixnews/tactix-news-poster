MCDM Unveils Survival Horror TTRPG 'Crows'

2026-08-23

MCDM has formally revealed Crows, an upcoming tabletop roleplaying game anchored in the atmospheric pressure of classic survival horror. Moving away from the cinematic power fantasy of Draw Steel, the newly announced system reframes dungeon exploration around claustrophobic tension, procedural risk, and acute scarcity.

Mechanically, Crows leans into design sensibilities reminiscent of Darkest Dungeon and early Resident Evil. The core loop elevates logistical friction into a primary tactical layer, relying on tactile, spatial inventory management. Players must deliberate over every item slot, balancing defensive utility against basic necessities like light sources and medical supplies where every expedition incurs a compounding cost.

This marks a sharp and calculated pivot for the studio. By positioning survival and inventory economy as the engine of dramatic play rather than secondary bookkeeping, MCDM is exploring a deliberate return to attrition-based design—where survival is earned through methodical logistics rather than raw heroic momentum.
