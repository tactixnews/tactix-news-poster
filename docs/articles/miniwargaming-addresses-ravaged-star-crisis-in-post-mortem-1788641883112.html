MiniWarGaming Addresses Ravaged Star Crisis in Post-Mortem

2026-09-05

MiniWarGaming co-founder Dave Hawkins has issued a candid post-mortem regarding the protracted fulfillment collapse of the Ravaged Star miniatures project. In an unusual display of public accountability within the hobby sector, Hawkins took full personal responsibility for the catastrophic supply chain breakdowns that stalled the ambitious sci-fi range, confirming that the studio will refocus resources on its core battle report production.

Ravaged Star originally promised premium plastic miniatures at direct-to-consumer scale, attempting to rival established industry giants without their entrenched logistical footprint. Instead, the initiative collided with the harsh realities of global manufacturing, tooling delays, and runaway freight overheads. For content-driven creators, managing physical supply lines requires capital and operational discipline that media studios are rarely structured to absorb.

The fallout serves as a sober case study for the crowdfunding era of tabletop gaming. As production margins compress and global logistics remain volatile, raw ambition cannot substitute for industrial expertise. MiniWarGaming's retreat to media highlights an enduring strategic truth: mastering the camera does not translate to mastering the supply line.
