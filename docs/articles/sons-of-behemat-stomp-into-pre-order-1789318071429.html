Sons of Behemat Stomp Into Pre-Order

2026-09-13

Games Workshop has officially opened pre-orders for an extensive Sons of Behemat update, delivering a reinforced foundation to one of Warhammer Age of Sigmar's most distinct, low-model-count factions. The wave introduces new elite sculpts, a focused army box, and a standalone giant-versus-giant duelling game designed to simulate gargant combat in close quarters.

For competitive Age of Sigmar commanders, Sons of Behemat have always presented a unique tactical puzzle. Operating with minimal activations forces players to optimize every phase of movement and engagement, where a single mispositioned titan can surrender critical objective control. This release refines their roster with specialized options, giving commanders the leverage needed to absorb high-rend assault packages without stalling on secondary scoring.

Alongside the colossal release, Games Workshop rolled out fresh Battlehost boxes for the Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game. The parallel releases highlight a broader strategic push: streamlining entry points for established narrative systems while expanding niche, high-concept playstyles that challenge traditional matched play assumptions.
