Blade Runner TTRPG Expansion Unveils Replicant Rebellion

2026-08-09

Free League Publishing has detailed Replicant Rebellion, a major expansion for the Blade Runner tabletop roleplaying game. Rather than placing players in the trench coats of LAPD Rep-Detectives tasked with hunting down rogue synthetic humans, this upcoming sourcebook inverts the core dynamic. Players instead take on the roles of underground operatives coordinating a covert campaign for replicant liberation across 2037 Los Angeles.

The expansion introduces dedicated frameworks for insurgency, covert operations, and network building across the dystopian metropolis. Alongside new character archetypes designed for subversion and stealth, Replicant Rebellion provides Game Runners with mechanical systems for managing operational security, heat, and executing coordinated strikes against corporate giants like the Wallace Corporation.

By shifting the lens from systemic enforcement to asymmetrical resistance, Free League is changing the tactical tension at the table. Success no longer hinges on forensic authority, but on resource management, moral compromise, and survival against overwhelming odds. For tabletop strategists, it offers a compelling evolution of the game's neon-noir DNA.
