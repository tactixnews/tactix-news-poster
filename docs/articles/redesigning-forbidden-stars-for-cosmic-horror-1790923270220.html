Redesigning Forbidden Stars for Cosmic Horror

2026-10-02

Samuel Bailey, original co-designer of Warhammer 40,000: Forbidden Stars, is reshaping the celebrated strategy title for a new cosmic horror setting. The project revisits the 2015 out-of-print classic, aiming to preserve its signature order-stacking engine while overhauling systemic friction points for modern table dynamics.

At the core of Forbidden Stars was a masterclass in predictive tempo: a last-in, first-out command system that made spatial bluffing as decisive as raw fleet power. Translating that architecture from grimdark attrition to asymmetric Lovecraftian entities demands significant recalibration. Bailey's revisions specifically target the original game's prolonged combat resolution, streamlining dense tactical engagements to sustain forward momentum without sacrificing player agency.

When Fantasy Flight Games and Games Workshop severed ties in 2017, Forbidden Stars was stranded as an orphaned masterpiece. Rebuilding its foundation around cosmic horror is more than an aesthetic pivot; it is an ambitious test of whether precision maneuver warfare can evoke existential dread as effectively as orbital bombardment.
