Discworld RPG Expands Into Lancre and Überwald

2026-09-12

Modiphius Entertainment is charting new territory across Terry Pratchett's Discworld. Following its initial focus on the cobblestones and institutional satire of Ankh-Morpork, the publisher has announced Discworld: Adventures in Lancre and Überwald, a standalone tabletop roleplaying game slated to launch on Gamefound this October.

The shift in geography represents a deliberate tonal evolution. Leaving behind the urban grit of the City Watch, the new title pivots toward the folk horror, gothic melodrama, and rustic pragmatism native to the Ramtop Mountains. For players and game masters, this transition swaps municipal investigations for high-stakes folklore, framing campaigns around aristocratic vampires, conflicted werewolves, and the sharp, headology-driven diplomacy of Lancre's witch covens.

Positioning the project as a standalone game rather than a modular supplement is a shrewd design choice. It lowers the barrier to entry for enthusiasts drawn specifically to Pratchett's rural storytelling while granting Modiphius the mechanical leeway to build rulesets tailored to isolated communities and gothic wilderness. How cleanly those systems evoke the balance between genuine dread and Pratchett's signature wit will be the critical test come autumn.
