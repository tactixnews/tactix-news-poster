Ogor Mawtribes Model Range Goes to Pre-Order

2026-08-16

Games Workshop has opened standalone pre-orders for the refreshed Ogor Mawtribes miniature roster in Warhammer Age of Sigmar. Previously locked behind a limited army set release, the updated sculpts for the Destruction grand alliance are now individually accessible to the wider wargaming community.

The refreshed kits modernize long-standing models, delivering dynamic frontline bruisers, support elements, and towering cavalry with contemporary scale and detail. For collectors and competitive players alike, standalone availability eliminates the supply bottleneck of bundled releases, streamlining targeted list building for the current season.

Strategically, the broader rollout gives Destruction generals greater flexibility to refine their shock-assault archetypes. Accessible core units and heavy hitters will allow tacticians to experiment with varied battalion structures and field fully updated forces without compromise.
