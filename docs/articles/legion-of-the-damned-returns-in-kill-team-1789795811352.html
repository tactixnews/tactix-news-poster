Legion of the Damned Returns in Kill Team

2026-09-19

Games Workshop has confirmed the return of the Legion of the Damned, reviving the enigmatic faction through an upcoming Kill Team expansion titled Armageddon. After years relegated to legacy rules and out-of-print metal miniatures, the flame-wreathed spectral Space Marines are officially receiving their first dedicated multipart plastic kits.

The expansion shifts Kill Team's operational theater back to the iconic ash wastes of Armageddon, a setting deeply rooted in the franchise's strategic lore. Reintroducing the Legion within a skirmish environment aligns with Nottingham's recent design philosophy: using Kill Team's smaller footprint to field specialized, boutique forces without immediately upending the balance of full-scale Warhammer 40,000 army lists.

From a systems perspective, an elite, supernatural operative roster offers compelling tactical trade-offs in high-attrition firefights. While Games Workshop has yet to reveal whether this release signals a wider integration into mainstream Adeptus Astartes doctrine, it underscores Kill Team's role as the primary incubator for modernized legacy models and experimental squad design.
