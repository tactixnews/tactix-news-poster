Wizards of the Coast Workers Launch Union Effort

2026-10-08

Employees across Wizards of the Coast, spanning flagship properties Magic: The Gathering and Dungeons & Dragons, have publicly announced their intent to form a union. The collective organizing drive covers staff across both tabletop production and digital operations, establishing the most high-profile labor movement in hobby gaming history.

The push arrives after years of sustained financial performance for Hasbro's premier division, set against an industry climate marked by restructuring, digital pivots, and recurring corporate layoffs. Wizards has long driven the commercial baseline for modern tabletop, yet the creative and technical workers sustaining those margins are now seeking formalized bargaining power over compensation, workloads, and job security.

While unionization has gained steady momentum in digital game development, the tabletop sector has historically operated on decentralized, often precarious labor structures. A successful campaign inside the market's dominant publisher sets a profound precedent—one likely to influence expectations across miniature design studios, board game houses, and independent RPG publishers worldwide.
