Classic Dungeon Crawler HeroQuest Headed to Steam

2026-10-17

HeroQuest is officially heading to Steam, bringing the foundational 1989 dungeon crawler to PC in a dedicated digital format. The adaptation aims to recreate the classic grid-based exploration and asymmetric hero-versus-overlord combat that originally cemented the game as a tabletop staple.

First co-developed by Milton Bradley and Games Workshop, HeroQuest served as an essential gateway between accessible family board games and the deeper tactical systems of tabletop roleplaying. Its minimalist dice combat, modular quest layouts, and tactile miniature presence established design conventions that modern heavyweights like Gloomhaven and Descent still iterate on decades later.

A digital transition offers obvious practical advantages, notably automated bookkeeping and instant matchmaking for solitary or remote play. However, the system's enduring appeal has always relied on the tense social friction between the adventuring party and Zargon across a physical table. Whether the PC release leans into faithful preservation or modern rules streamlining, it places a cornerstone of dungeon design before a completely new generation of strategists.
