Space Marine Codex Moves 16 Units to Legends

2026-08-25

Games Workshop has confirmed a significant roster adjustment for the upcoming Codex: Space Marines, transitioning 16 legacy datasheets out of matched play and into Warhammer Legends. Alongside the competitive retirement of these classic kits, the studio unveiled a modernized Space Marine Captain on Bike designed to anchor Outrider-heavy vanguard forces.

The pruning represents a deliberate effort to streamline the historically dense Space Marine roster. By retiring older firstborn variants and redundant wargear profiles to casual play, the studio is sharpening the faction's identity around its current range. For competitive commanders, the shift eliminates several niche rules interactions and forces an immediate re-evaluation of fast-attack and transport slots.

While the departure of long-standing options always challenges veteran collections, the redesigned mounted Captain indicates that specific archetypes are receiving focused, intentional updates. A leaner datasheet ecosystem may sting in the short term, but it delivers much-needed clarity to list construction across the 41st millennium.
