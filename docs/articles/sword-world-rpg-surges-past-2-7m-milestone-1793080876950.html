Sword World RPG Surges Past $2.7M Milestone

2026-10-27

The official English localization of Sword World 2.5 has crossed $2.7 million on BackerKit, turning an overdue translation project into one of the most lucrative tabletop campaigns to date. With nearly a month remaining in its funding cycle, the drive demonstrates an extraordinary Western appetite for Japan's foundational fantasy roleplaying system.

First released in 1989 alongside the influential Record of Lodoss War franchise, Sword World established the mechanical and thematic vocabulary for Japanese tabletop gaming. For three and a half decades, while Dungeons & Dragons held court in the West, Sword World thrived as an Asian benchmark, defined by its streamlined 2d6 resolution engine, brisk narrative pacing, and deep character customization.

The velocity of this campaign signals a broader inflection point across the hobby. Tabletop players are increasingly looking beyond domestic design orthodoxy, gravitating toward established international systems with historical weight. Sword World is no longer just an import curiosity—it is shaping up to be a permanent fixture in the global roleplaying ecosystem.
