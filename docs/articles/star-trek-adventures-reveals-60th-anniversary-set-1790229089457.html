Star Trek Adventures Reveals 60th Anniversary Set

2026-09-24

Modiphius Entertainment has unveiled a dedicated 60th Anniversary Collector's Boxed Set for Star Trek Adventures, marking six decades of the science fiction franchise. The premium package consolidates the studio's 2d20 system core rule set alongside bespoke accessories and curated campaign materials designed to serve as a centerpiece for long-running tables.

Since its debut, Star Trek Adventures has stood out as a sophisticated translation of on-screen ethos into tabletop mechanics. The system's Momentum and Threat economies capture the friction between Starfleet idealism and deep-space volatility, prioritizing scientific inquiry and collaborative problem-solving over simple attrition. This commemorative edition packages those systems with archival-grade presentation tailored to dedicated bridge crews.

Milestone editions in the TTRPG space often lean heavily on collector novelty, but this release reinforces the enduring viability of licensed, narrative-forward design. By matching luxury physical components with an established, mechanically sound engine, Modiphius continues to treat the franchise not merely as merchandise, but as a compelling framework for strategic roleplay.
