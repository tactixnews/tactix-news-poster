D&D Announces Star Wars and Warcraft Expansions

2026-07-31

Wizards of the Coast has announced that its high-profile Universes Beyond initiative is officially expanding into Dungeons & Dragons, introducing licensed campaign settings and rulebooks for Star Wars and World of Warcraft.

The move applies Hasbro’s successful cross-franchise licensing strategy—previously restricted to Magic: The Gathering—directly to its flagship tabletop RPG. By porting these massive external IPs into the fifth edition framework, Wizards aims to draw established sci-fi and gaming audiences into the TTRPG ecosystem while broadening the mechanical scope of the system.

Integrating sci-fi technology, Force abilities, and MMO-style class mechanics into D&D's d20 system presents a distinct design challenge. For the broader industry, this expansion signals a shift toward viewing core TTRPG engines as adaptable operating systems for global media franchises.
