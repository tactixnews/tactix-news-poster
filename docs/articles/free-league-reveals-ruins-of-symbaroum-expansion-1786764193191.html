Free League Reveals Ruins of Symbaroum Expansion

2026-08-15

Free League Publishing has officially announced The Throne of Thorns II, the next major chapter in its Ruins of Symbaroum campaign line adapted for the 5th Edition ruleset. Pre-orders are now live on the publisher's webshop, granting backers immediate access to early digital alpha rules.

The expansion advances the narrative deeper into the untamed interior of the Davokar forest, shifting the mechanical and narrative focus toward high-stakes factional politics, resource scarcity, and lethal environmental hazards. Where conventional 5E campaigns often lean into heroic invulnerability, Ruins of Symbaroum deliberately re-engineers familiar d20 mechanics to emphasize corruption, tactical risk assessment, and severe systemic consequences.

By marrying Free League's signature dark fantasy atmosphere with robust 5E design, The Throne of Thorns II reinforces the publisher's broader strategic push: offering mainstream tabletop players an uncompromising, mechanically grounded alternative to traditional high fantasy.
