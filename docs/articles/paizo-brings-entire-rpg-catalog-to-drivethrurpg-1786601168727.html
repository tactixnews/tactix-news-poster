Paizo Brings Entire RPG Catalog to DriveThruRPG

2026-08-13

Publisher Paizo has expanded its digital distribution by making its complete product catalog available on DriveThruRPG. The rollout brings thousands of titles—including Pathfinder First and Second Editions, Starfinder, and decades of legacy adventure paths—to tabletop gaming's largest digital storefront.

Historically, Paizo prioritized direct-to-consumer sales through its own webstore, relying on proprietary subscriptions and native PDF delivery. While that storefront remains active, making the entire catalog available on DriveThruRPG aligns with modern player habits. Tabletop gamers increasingly favor single-library convenience for file management, third-party VTT integration, and centralized digital collections.

This expansion represents a calculated strategy to lower friction for new players while cementing Paizo’s footprint. By placing its extensive rules system alongside the broader indie ecosystem, Paizo strengthens its position at a time when digital accessibility directly dictates market share in the TTRPG landscape.
