My Hero Academia Minis Game Announced at Gen Con

2026-08-01

Catalyst Game Labs unveiled a pre-painted miniatures game set in the My Hero Academia universe at Gen Con, alongside hands-on previews of its upcoming tactical skirmish title, Gundam Assemble.

Anime intellectual properties have traditionally struggled to establish lasting roots in the competitive miniatures space, frequently prioritizing surface nostalgia over enduring game design. Catalyst’s decision to launch My Hero Academia with pre-painted figures indicates a calculated attempt to eliminate the barrier of hobby assembly while introducing a mainstream fanbase to grid-based tactical engagement.

Simultaneously, early hands-on impressions of Gundam Assemble signal a broader portfolio evolution for the veteran publisher. Best known for the simulation-heavy crunch of BattleTech, Catalyst is actively expanding its footprint toward fast-paced, IP-driven skirmish systems. Whether these new titles can offer the strategic sophistication required for lasting competitive play remains the key metric to watch.
