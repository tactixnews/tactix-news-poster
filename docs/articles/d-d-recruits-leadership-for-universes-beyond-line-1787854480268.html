D&D Recruits Leadership for Universes Beyond Line

2026-08-27

Hasbro is recruiting senior creative leadership to build a dedicated crossover product line for Dungeons & Dragons, directly adapting the commercial framework pioneered by Magic: The Gathering's Universes Beyond.

For Magic, third-party collaborations spanning Warhammer 40,000 to The Lord of the Rings transformed external intellectual property into massive commercial drivers. Bringing this blueprint to tabletop roleplaying signals a clear shift toward licensed, multi-franchise sourcebooks built natively on D&D mechanics.

While the game has occasionally touched outside properties through one-off digital partnerships, establishing a permanent leadership team points to an ongoing publishing pipeline. The strategic challenge will be structural: translating wildly diverse cinematic and gaming universes into a ruleset historically anchored in medieval fantasy.
