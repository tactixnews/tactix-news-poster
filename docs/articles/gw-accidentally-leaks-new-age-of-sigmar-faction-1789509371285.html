GW Accidentally Leaks New Age of Sigmar Faction

2026-09-15

Brief product listings on a regional Games Workshop webstore have prematurely revealed a new dwarf faction for Warhammer Age of Sigmar: the Khazalite Holds. While the entries were swiftly pulled offline, the slip confirms that an expansion to the Grand Alliance of Order is officially imminent.

The emergence of the Khazalite Holds points toward a long-awaited tactical recalibration for the duardin in the Mortal Realms. Dispossessed collections have occupied an ambiguous space for years, caught between the aerial mobility of the Kharadron Overlords and the close-quarters aggression of the Fyreslayers. A dedicated Khazalite release suggests Games Workshop aims to re-anchor classic duardin aesthetics within contemporary design frameworks.

From a meta perspective, the addition will force Order generals to reconsider their battlelines. How these units balance classic defensive grit, potential runic mechanics, and heavy infantry will determine whether the Holds forge a genuine strategic niche or merely overlap with existing duardin archetypes. With the digital footprint exposed, an official reveal should follow shortly.
