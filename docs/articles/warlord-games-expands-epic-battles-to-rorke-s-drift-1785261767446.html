Warlord Games Expands Epic Battles to Rorke’s Drift

2026-07-28

Warlord Games is bringing its Black Powder Epic Battles system to the 1879 Anglo-Zulu War, announcing a new boxed set centered on the siege of Rorke's Drift. The upcoming release translates one of history's most famous tactical standoffs into the publisher's signature micro-scale format, complete with period-accurate fortifications, scenario rules, and hundreds of miniature figures.

The transition to Epic Battles scale represents a deliberate mechanical shift for historical wargaming. By shrinking figure dimensions, Warlord enables tabletop strategists to render full-scale battlefield logistics and troop densities that are usually impossible at standard 28mm scales. For a defense as spatially compact and desperate as Rorke's Drift, this scale emphasizes line-of-sight discipline and perimeter management over individual heroics.

Expanding Black Powder into distinct historical theaters demonstrates Warlord’s commitment to lowering the barrier for mass-battle systems. By bundling bespoke terrain alongside massive unit counts in a single box, the company continues to refine the entry point for historical wargamers seeking grand tactical depth without the traditional hobby friction of assembling thousands of full-sized miniatures.
