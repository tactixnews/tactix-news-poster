Games Workshop Abruptly Scraps Necromunda RPG

2026-10-24

Games Workshop has unexpectedly shelved its planned Necromunda roleplaying game, abruptly terminating the project barely a month after its public announcement. The decision brings an immediate halt to the underhive-focused tabletop system before any core mechanics, sourcebooks, or release windows were disclosed.

A dedicated Necromunda RPG represented a significant strategic opportunity: translating the dense, vertical skirmish setting of the 41st millennium into an intimate, narrative-first format. While Warhammer 40,000 maintains active roleplaying lines like Wrath & Glory and Imperium Maledictum via licensing partner Cubicle 7, Necromunda's distinct street-level gang warfare offered a gritty criminal niche entirely distinct from inquisitorial intrigue or high-stakes military campaigns.

The sudden reversal leaves the underhive confined entirely to its skirmish miniatures format for the foreseeable future. For players eager to explore the industrial depths through pure roleplay, this quiet cancellation suggests either an abrupt internal realignment or a reluctance to fracture focus across an already crowded tabletop roleplaying slate.
