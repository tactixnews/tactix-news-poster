Custodes Codex Overhauls Rules, Retires Seven Units

2026-09-26

Games Workshop has previewed sweeping systemic changes for the Adeptus Custodes ahead of their upcoming codex, pairing wide-ranging datasheet revisions with the retirement of seven unit profiles to Legends status. The shift immediately excludes those options from matched play, narrowing the faction's competitive roster while redefining its core mechanical identity.

For Custodes generals, the reclassification marks a critical inflection point in list construction. Historically reliant on niche datasheets to cover mobility gaps and anti-armor deficiencies, competitive pilots must now lean into updated army rules and reworked detachment mechanics. The loss of versatility places greater strategic emphasis on pure positioning discipline and maximizing the efficiency of the Emperor's core infantry.

Trimming legacy options reflects a broader design pattern across Warhammer 40,000's current edition: standardizing unit pools to sharpen internal codex balance. While losing long-standing models stings for veteran collectors, the revised datasheets promise a tighter, more cohesive tactical framework for the golden host on the tabletop.
